## Record

The card says this checkpoint is Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct fine-tuned on the MIS training set. That set is Tuwhy/MIS_Train, 3,927 samples. The dataset card says the safety chain-of-thought labels were generated by InternVL2.5-78B. The Hub dataset is public and the viewer shows those rows. The paper also says the authors added 500 general question-answer samples. Those 500 are not a separate Hub dataset.

The paper calls the method multi-image multimodal supervised fine-tuning. For Qwen2-VL it uses LlamaFactory and says the runs are full-parameter fine-tuning of the language-model backbone and the projector. Table 7 lists the Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct MIRage row as learning rate 2e-6, warmup ratio 0.03, and 3 epochs. No source states a LoRA rank, a batch size, or a step count. The repository ships four safetensors shards and no adapter file. GitHub has inference scripts, not a training script.

The card and the paper both name the base Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct. The GitHub README links `https://huggingface.co/Tuwhy/Qwen2-VL-7B-MIRage` as the Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct model fine-tuned with MIRage. The paper names that run Qwen2-VL-7B+MIRage. The card calls the model a 7B Qwen2-VL.

The card does not present a therapist, counselor, or support chatbot. It is a vision-language model whose chat template says "You are a helpful assistant" and whose training targets a safe reply to a question plus images. That is a chatbot. There is no therapy or medical-use warning. The paper says the method "remains vulnerable to certain adversarial attacks." On the MIS test set, the paper's Qwen2-VL-7B+MIRage row has attack-success rates of 1.67, 1.76, and 1.00 on the easy, hard, and real splits. License is Apache 2.0.

The inputs are images plus text. The dataset field is a list of images, and the paper says the risk comes from "text instructions linking objects across two images." `generation_config.json` sets `do_sample` true, temperature 0.1, top_p 0.001, top_k 1, and repetition_penalty 1.05. `max_position_embeddings` is 32768.
