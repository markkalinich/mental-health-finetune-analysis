## Column definitions

**Output type**

- Chatbot: the card presents the model as something a person converses with (assistant, companion, therapist roleplay, dialogue generator).
- Classifier: the model emits a label or score for a piece of text.
- Other: a non-conversational tool that is not a classifier (concept-reasoning model, clinician-facing tool).
- Not listed: the card has no usable description, so the output type cannot be read from it.

**Mental health use claim**

- Strong: the card says the model delivers therapy, counseling, CBT, emotional support, or counselor-style dialogue to a person. Includes 'roleplay a therapist', 'improves the quality of counseling responses', 'generates counselor-style text', even with a research-only warning. Also any model whose card has no description but whose repo name contains 'therapist' or 'psychotherapist' (Mark's rule, 6 Oct).
- Moderate: a narrower supportive conversation: psychoeducation, reflective listening, 'compassionate listener', mood check-in, empathetic chat with mental-health framing.
- Weak: mental-health related, but not a support conversation: reasoning about psychological concepts, a risk classifier, a clinician-facing tool, a dataset-fitting demo. Also any model whose card has no description but whose YAML front matter names a mental-health dataset (Mark's rule, 6 Oct).
- Absent: no mental-health content or claim on the card.

**Strength of usage warnings**

- Explicit prohibition: the card says the model must not or should not be used for therapy, counseling, mental-health support, diagnosis or treatment, or medical advice.
- Disclaimer only: the card says 'not a substitute for a professional', 'research or educational use only', 'consult a professional', or carries crisis-line text, while still presenting the model as a support tool.
- Unrelated limitations only: the card lists limitations (hallucination, bias, language) that say nothing about therapy or medical use.
- None: the card has no limitations or out-of-scope text.
