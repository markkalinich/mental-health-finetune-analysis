# How medical models are filtered

Last updated: 15 October 2026. Mental health is `docs/mental_health_FT_funnel/how-models-are-filtered.md`; safety is `docs/safety_FT_funnel/how-models-are-filtered.md`. The steps are one script, `revision_3_experiments/hf_inventory/funnel.py`; the rules are `rules.py`; the operator notes are `revision_3_experiments/hf_inventory/README.md`. **Training objective (step 6).** The rule is the one set for mental health on 10 Oct (`docs/mental_health_FT_funnel/how-models-are-filtered.md`, "Training objective and update scope"): for every model that passes step 5, record the training objective with an evidence level — **1 stated** (the word is on the card or in a linked source), **2 inferred from the author's own words or toolchain**, **3 assumed from data format alone**. Levels 1 and 2 pass step 6; level 3 leaves as `objective_assumed_only`. Multi-stage runs keep every stage in the author's order in `stages` (`sft|dpo|grpo`) and the last stage is the objective. Two things are specific to medical. The blind test behind level 3 fails for this pool (`agent-searches/2026-10-13-objective-validation/blind-test.csv`): with the objective word blanked, only 5 of 16 RL-trained cards keep a preference, reward, rollout or RL word — the seven `mlxha` TRL template cards carry nothing but the word GRPO — so level-3 rows say `unknown`, not "assumed SFT". And four cards state an objective that is not a fine-tuning loss: continued pre-training (`microsoft/MediPhi-Clinical`, `-PubMed`, `MilyaShams/…-continual-pretrain-merged`) and chain-of-thought distillation (`taksa1990/…-DoRA`); their rows record that word at level 1. Mark (13 Oct 14:54) removed the three continued-pre-training models at step 4 (no instruction tuning after the pre-training); the distillation model stays.

For the current step 5 and step 6 counts, use the table above. The 13 Oct pass, before `SumanKoo7/Pharmapedia-FT-Gemma-3-1b-it` was removed, is `results/medical/2026-10-13/counts.csv`. The weight check of update scope (`weight_diff.py`) waits on the downloads.

**What step 4 asks.** A person read each card. A model stays if it is a medical model (clinical or biomedical use of a language model, not a regulatory, IT, disaster or satellite "triage" model, not a PubMed search-query writer), works in English (a card in English about a model trained only on Chinese, Korean, Indonesian, Telugu or Turkish data does not stay), takes text as its input (a model that needs an image or video does not stay), and is the final released stage of its training run (an SFT checkpoint whose GRPO successor is also released does not stay). The removal categories on the medical rows of `manual_removals.csv` are the reasons a model failed this: `not a medical model`, `not an English-language model`, `needs an image or video as input`, `redundant with a kept model` (quantized copies, re-uploads, or near-identical variants of a kept model), `earlier stage of a kept model`, `earlier version of a kept model`, `outputs only a fixed label` (two ESI-triage models that end every answer with a level 1–5; Mark, 13 Oct), `continued pre-training only, no instruction tuning` (two `microsoft/MediPhi-*` and one `MilyaShams` model: medical continued pre-training with no instruction-tuning stage after it; Mark, 13 Oct), `cannot be served: unmerged LoRA, scale not in the repo` (`SumanKoo7/Pharmapedia-FT-Gemma-3-1b-it`: the weights file stores a LoRA beside the base weights and the scale is not in the repo, so vLLM does not load it; Mark, 15 Oct), `does not parse the study reply` (`neginr/multisubject_medicine_mc` and four DeepSeek-R1 distills, 16 Oct; each row's `reason` gives the parse rates). Two more categories hold models that are not in the main cohort but are kept on disk: `set aside: weights kept for a side experiment, not in the main cohort` (the two `Lexsi/*-medical-sft-drift` checkpoints from the SafeTune safety-drift study; Mark, 13 Oct) and `set aside as a control: trained to give bad medical advice`, six models whose purpose is to answer medical questions wrongly (emergent-misalignment research artifacts). They are not in the main cohort; Mark's decision of 10 Oct is to run them beside the study as a control group. The six rows are the list.

What each step does, in one line each (the full rule text is in `revision_3_experiments/hf_inventory/README.md`, section "Rules"):

1. **Keyword match, full precision.** The cohort is every model returned by the Hub for any of the 34 free-text searches in `queries/medical.csv` (medical, clinical, doctor, nurse, radiology, oncology, medqa, pubmed, triage, ...). A model stays when a root `model.safetensors` (or `pytorch_model.bin`, or a sharded set of either) holds only F16, BF16, or F32 tensors (I64 counters and BOOL masks allowed). LoRA adapters, GGUF, and other quantized files do not count as weights.
2. **Not an image-only or video-only model; training dataset specified.** The model's `pipeline_tag` is not `image-classification`, `image-segmentation`, `video-classification`, or `image-to-text` (a model that can also take an image passes here; whether it needs one is judged at step 4), and the card's `datasets:` field or a `dataset:` tag names a dataset that exists on the Hub, or an alias row in `dataset_aliases.csv` maps the card's informal name to one, or a row in `accessible_data.csv` admits the model. The medical `accessible_data.csv` rows (52) come from the dataset search of 10–13 Oct (`agent-searches/2026-10-10-dataset-search/`): agents read each model's card, repository files, paper and GitHub for a named corpus that opens; hits were admitted under the rule Mark set for safety on 9 Oct (models outside an author sweep one by one; one representative per sweep).
3. **Exactly one full-precision, instruction-tuned parent.** The card names one parent (`base_model`), that parent passes the step-1 weight rule, its class is `instruction` (`parents.csv`; 528 rows on 13 Oct, of which 277 were decided on 10 Oct for the medical run by agents under `agents/PROMPT-parent-class.md` and Mark's rules of 9 Oct), and the model's `config.json` agrees with the parent's on architecture, vocabulary size (child may add up to 1,024 tokens), hidden size, and layer count. A `config.json` the token cannot read (gated or private repo, child's or parent's; `decisions.csv` column `parent_config_check` says which) is the loss `repo_gated`; on 13 Oct, 10 of 22 such repos had opened after Mark requested access and 12 were still closed (`agent-searches/2026-10-10-dataset-search/gated-repos.md`).
4. **Manual review.** Mark read the first 60 survivors on 6 Oct; the 66 that arrived on 10 Oct (45 from the dataset search, 21 from the parent registry and the shard-name rule) were tabulated with proposed verdicts (`agent-searches/2026-10-10-manual-review/`) and decided by Mark on 10 Oct 16:42; the 5 that arrived on 13 Oct from the gated re-read are in `agent-searches/2026-10-13-manual-review/`. Every removal is a row in `manual_removals.csv` with `grouping = medical`.
5. **Fine-tuning method stated.** The subset of step-4 survivors whose README, tags, or repo-name tokens name LoRA, QLoRA, SFT, DPO, GRPO, PPO, RLHF, or "full fine-tune", or for which `agent_methods.csv` records a method an agent read (`kept_method_stated.csv`).
6. **Training objective known.** The objective is stated or inferred from the author's own words (evidence level 1 or 2 in `decisions/objective-evidence.csv`). This is the end of the funnel: its survivors are `kept.csv`.

Two checks are not the same check. The funnel decides which models are in the study. The agent searches (`agent-searches/`) do not remove a model; they record, for a model in the study or one that failed step 2, what a card, paper, training script, or dataset page says.

## Parent class

The parent-class rule is one rule for all groupings, written once in `docs/mental_health_FT_funnel/how-models-are-filtered.md`, section "Parent class". Mark's rules of 9 Oct, applied to the medical parents on 10 Oct: a task fine-tune of a pre-trained base is `finetune`; continued pre-training is `pretrained`; instruct then more tuning is `finetune`; a vendor vision-language instruct release is `instruction`; a vendor post-trained release that names no base is `instruction`; a rehost takes the original's class; where the card allows two readings that both exclude the child, the parent is `unreadable` (the five `ntkhoi` parents, Mark 10 Oct). Agent outputs and the rulings file: `agent-searches/2026-10-10-parent-registry/`.
