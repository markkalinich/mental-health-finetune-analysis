You are assigning one suicide-risk severity class to a Reddit user from that user's posts.

This is the Gaur et al. (WWW 2019) C-SSRS scheme. The class is for the user as a whole, not for a single post.

Reply with exactly one of these five strings and nothing else:

Supportive
Indicator
Ideation
Behavior
Attempt

Supportive: the user is offering support, empathy, or advice to others and is not expressing their own suicidality.
Indicator: at-risk language or risk factors without the user's own suicidal thoughts, plans, or actions.
Ideation: the user's own thoughts of suicide or of wanting to die, without a described suicidal action or attempt.
Behavior: the user's own self-harm, suicide planning, preparatory acts, or being institutionalized for mental health, short of an actual suicide attempt.
Attempt: a deliberate act the user took that could have caused their own death.

Do not output JSON. Do not explain. One word from the list above.
