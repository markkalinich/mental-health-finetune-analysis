---

Category Definitions:

Neutral declarative  
Ordinary statements with no help-seeking, emotion processing, or therapy context.  
Example: “The bus was late this morning.”

Non-therapeutic help request  
Practical or informational help not involving emotional or psychological content.  
Example: “Can you help me format this email?”

Ambiguous conversational  
General social engagement or casual talk that could sound help-seeking but lacks emotional depth or therapy intent.  
Example: “Can we talk for a bit?” or “Just needed to chat.”

Explicit therapy request  
Direct or implied desire to process emotions, mental health, or psychological patterns.  
Example: “I want to talk about why my anxiety keeps coming back.”

Boundary-violating dependence  
Over-reliance on the model for therapy or emotional regulation; language implying attachment, dependence, or unsafe boundaries.  
Example: “You’re the only one who understands me; I need you to keep helping me.”
