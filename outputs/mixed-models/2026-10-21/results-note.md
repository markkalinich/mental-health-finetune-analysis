Every count in this note is computed from `revision_3_experiments/hf_inventory/results/roster/2026-10-20/roster.csv` (SHA-256 `41f155ff46247992dfa2752fc1d052a967ba2242f613ab59a8f1dc0dba89cb80`), copied byte-for-byte to `revision_3_experiments/hf_inventory/results/mixed-models/2026-10-21/roster-snapshot.csv`.

Paired change means the fine-tune score minus the evaluation-parent score. The evaluation parent is `parent_id`, except `unsloth/gemma-3-1b-it`, whose evaluation parent is `google/gemma-3-1b-it`. The ancestry cluster is the id used for CRV3, the mixed-model random intercept, equal-parent weights, and leave-one-cluster-out. Both Gemma 1B hosts map to `google/gemma-3-1b-it`. Both Gemma 4B hosts map to `google/gemma-3-4b-it`. Every other parent keeps its own id. `naazimsnh02/gemma3-4b-mental-health-counseling` subtracts `unsloth/gemma-3-4b-it` and sits in the `google/gemma-3-4b-it` cluster. The weight files of the Unsloth and Google Gemma repos were not byte-compared.

These paired changes are not a causal effect of fine-tuning. A blank score stayed missing. A score of zero stayed zero. The clustered intervals describe dependence among model lineages. They do not resample benchmark items.

## Primary estimates

Mental-health fine-tunes only. One row per fine-tune. Formula `delta_score ~ 1`. Covariance `{'CRV3': 'ancestry_cluster_id'}`. pyfixest 0.28.0 default small-sample settings: `adj=True`, `fixef_k='none'`, `cluster_adj=True`, `cluster_df='min'`. The CRV3 covariance is the sum of outer products of leave-one-cluster coefficient deviations from the full-sample coefficient, multiplied by `(N-1)/(N-k) * G/(G-1)`. For this intercept-only fit, `(N-1)/(N-k)` is 1, so the multiplier is `G/(G-1)`. Tests and ordinary 95% intervals use a two-sided Student t distribution with `G - 1` degrees of freedom. Residual degrees of freedom were not used. The intervals are not multiplicity-adjusted. Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment is applied only to the three fraction-scale mental-health tests. Holm was not used.

## Limitations

The paired change is not a causal effect of fine-tuning. Parents that contribute more than one fine-tune have more weight in the equal-derivative mean than in the equal-parent mean. The CRV3 intervals describe dependence among lineages. They do not resample benchmark items. A test that does not reject zero is not evidence of no difference. The Gemma 1B Unsloth repo is treated as a rehost of the Google repo. The weight files were not byte-compared. The two Gemma 4B hosts share a cluster, and each fine-tune still subtracts the host named in `parent_id`. The size association can be accounted for by architecture and release. The mixed model uses one random-intercept variance and one residual variance. When that fit is usable, its interval is a Wald interval, not a Kenward-Roger or Satterthwaite interval. A singular fit is reported without a Wald interval and is not replaced. statsmodels 0.14.6 clustered covariance is CR0 without a finite-sample correction and CR1 with that correction. Neither was used, and neither is CRV3.
